JAIN AUTO - FIXED STATUS VERSION

This version fixes the Owner Dashboard date behavior:
- Next opening time is OPTIONAL.
- Keep open until is OPTIONAL.
- OPEN NOW can be saved without choosing a closing time.
- CLOSE SHOP can be saved without choosing a next opening time.
- ON BREAK can be saved without either time.
- The Google Apps Script URL is already placed in config.js.

To use this version:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the new car4 folder.
3. Double-click index.html to test the customer site.
4. Open Owner Login and sign in with your current Google Sheet credentials.
5. Choose OPEN NOW, CLOSE SHOP, or ON BREAK.
6. Click Update Live Status.
7. Refresh the customer page.

Note: This ZIP does not change your Google Sheet or Apps Script deployment.
